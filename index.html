The job in one minute

New to hotel housekeeping? These are the only terms you need for this page.

Inspector (RQS)Checks each cleaned room before a guest gets it and leads a small team of housekeepers. This is Hari's role.
Full CleanA room cleaned top to bottom after a guest checks out. Every one is inspected.
Daily ServiceA lighter tidy-up for guests who are staying on.
Room list (chart)One housekeeper's rooms for the day. Building these lists every morning is a big planning job.
HotSOSThe hotel's system where each room is assigned to a housekeeper and its status is tracked.
SSRS reportThe hotel's daily report of which rooms need cleaning, and how.

What's inside the BGV Scheduler

Each part replaces something that was done by hand.

It plans by the department's own rules

The app follows the same rules an experienced supervisor would, every time.
  • No one gets more Full Cleans than fit in a working day.
  • Daily Service lists are filled to a full day before anyone is moved off Full Cleans.
  • Rooms in one apartment stay with one person, and buildings are not mixed on one list.
  • Rooms are listed in walking order, floor by floor.
  • Rooms with early arrivals are cleaned first.
  • At weekends, carpet cleaning for pet rooms and linen stripping are assigned automatically.

Sources

How we measured

Every measured number comes from the team's own records. Estimates are marked as estimates.

  • Rooms inspected: counted from every daily tab of the GC8 Inspections 2026 workbook, Jan 1 – Oct 14. A room counts once, for the first inspector named on it. Full Cleans only; Daily Service is not counted.
  • Days on the schedule and last-minute absences: the department's weekly staff schedule. "Full-time" means on the schedule 3.5 or more days a week, and still on the team.
  • Hours building: the time stamps of 366 saved changes in the three code projects on GitHub (BGV Scheduler, the first version of it, and GC8 Budget Tracker). Changes less than two hours apart count as one sitting, plus 30 minutes to get started. This is a floor, not the full total.
  • Project days vs personal time: a project day is a day whose entry on Hari's staff schedule mentions projects, the system or the websites (May 1 – Oct 15). Every other hour is counted as personal time, whether on a regular inspection day or a day off.
  • Use: the app's sign-in log, Sep 15 – Oct 15, 2026, counting colleagues only (Hari's two accounts left out), and its room-status records since live HotSOS tracking began on Oct 9.
  • Time and money saved: estimates of how long each task took by hand, and an assumed $25 an hour. Managers can adjust both.
